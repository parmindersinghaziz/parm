About

An academic, poet, and translator

Early Roots

Born on October 9, 1980 in Fazilka. At six, family moved to Abohar. Matriculated at Divine Light Public School with 81%, national merit list: 100 in Maths, 99 in Sanskrit, 98 in English.

Humanities & IT

Pursued BA in English via Panjab University, while enrolling at NIIT. By 18, was teaching at a computer centre. Later transferred to Chandigarh for advanced IT training and college.

Academic Transition

Mentored by Prof. D.K. Chatterjee, shifted after 12 years of IT industry work into academia. Now Assistant Professor of English at Dev Samaj College for Women, Chandigarh.

Research & Innovations

Research spans NEP 2020, digital pedagogy, Punjabiyat. His poetry embeds QR codes that link to audio recitations — a pioneering blend of print and technology.
